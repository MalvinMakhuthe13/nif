# NIF Capture Guide

## Best practices for a great NIF

### Video capture (recommended)
- Walk slowly around the subject — 10-30 seconds total
- Keep the subject centred in frame throughout
- Overlap each step by ~60% (slow movement)
- Keep lighting consistent — avoid moving from sun into shadow mid-capture
- Shoot at the highest resolution your phone allows (4K preferred)
- Avoid motion blur — hold the phone steady

### Photo burst
- Minimum 24 images, maximum 200
- Orbit the subject in a circle at consistent height
- Add a second row from a higher angle for better top coverage
- Format: JPG, PNG, HEIC

### LiDAR (iOS Pro / iPad Pro)
- Use the Files app to export the .las or .ply file
- Works best for architecture, furniture, vehicles

### Supported formats
Video: MP4, MOV, WebM, AVI
Photo: JPG, PNG, HEIC, RAW, DNG
Point cloud: LAS, LAZ, PTS, XYZ, PLY
