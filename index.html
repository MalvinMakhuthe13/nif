How it works

Four steps.
No expertise.

1
Film your subject
Walk slowly around it for 15–30 seconds. Any phone, any lighting. The more angles you cover, the richer the result.
2
Upload and reconstruct
Drop your video into the dashboard. Our GPU pipeline extracts depth, removes the background, and rebuilds the full 4D scene with real colour and geometry.
3
Edit and perfect
Open the scene editor. Isolate your subject, adjust lighting, set depth-of-field. Remove the background in one click. The Photoshop of spatial captures.
4
Share, embed, print
Share to any platform as a video. Embed interactively on any website with one script tag. Send to the 3D print pipeline and download your STL in minutes.
